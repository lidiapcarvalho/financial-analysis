# Financial Glossary

## Status

- **🟢 Familiar** - I recognise and understand the term with little difficulty
- **🟡 Learning** - I recognise the term but am still consolidating its meaning or use
- **🔴 Review** - I need to review or study the term further

## Financial Terms

| **English**            | **Português**                      | **Meaning / Context**                                                                     | **Status** |
|------------------------|------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----|
| Revenue                | Receita                            | Money generated from a company's business activities, before deducting expense.                  | 🟡 |
| Operating Income       | Resultado Operacional              | Profit generated from a company's core operations, before interest and taxes.                    | 🟡 |
| Net Income             | Resultado Líquido                  | Profit remaining after expenses, interest and taxes have been deducted                           | 🟡 |
| Total Assets           | Ativo Total                        | Everything of value owned or controlled by a company.                                            | 🟡 |
| Total Liabilities      | Passivo Total                      | Financial obligations and debts owmed by a company.                                              | 🟡 |
| Shareholders' Equity   | Capital Próprio                    | The value attributable to the company's shareholders after liabilities are deducted from assets. | 🟡 |
| Revenue Growth         | Crescimento da receita             | The increase or decrease in revenue over a given period.                                         | 🟡 |
| Operating Margin       | Margem Operacional                 | Operating income expressed as a percentage of revenue.                                           | 🟡 |
| Net Profit Margin      | Margem Líquida                     | Net income expressed as a percentage of revenue.                                                 | 🟡 |
| Return on Assets (ROA) | Rendabilidade Operacional do Ativo | A measure of how efficiently a company generates profit from its assets.                         | 🟢 |
| Return on Equity (ROE) | Rendabilidade do Capital Próprio   | A measure of how efficiently a company generates profit from shareholders' equity.               | 🟢 |
| Financial Performance  | Desempenho Financeiro              | How well a company perfoms financially over a given period.                                      | 🟢 |
| Reporting Currency     | Moeda de Apresentação              | The currency in which a company presents its financial statements.                               | 🟡 |
| Exchange Rate          | Taxa de Câmbio                     | The value of one currency expressed in terms of another curency.                                 | 🟢 |
| Currency Coversion     | Conversão de Moeda                 | The process of converting an amount from one currency into another.                              | 🟢 |
| Luxury Goods           | Bens de Luxo                       | High-end products associated with premium pricing, exclusivity, and brand value.                 | 🟢 |
| Diversified            | Diversificado                      | Describes a company with activities across diferent products, markets, or business segments.     | 🟢 |
| Conglomerate           | Conglomerado                       | A company or group that owns or operates businesses across different industries or segments.     | 🟡 |

## Notes

- `Revenue` is translated as **receita** in this project. Although "rendimento" may appear as a translation in some contexts, it is not the preferred term here.
- `Income` can have different meanings depending on the financial context. For example, `Net Income` and `Operating Income` do not translate simplys as "rendimento".
- Financial concepts and formulas are documented separately in [`financial-concepts.md`](financial-concepts.md).
